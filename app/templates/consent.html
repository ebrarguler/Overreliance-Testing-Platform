Research Participant Informed Consent Form

Please read this document carefully before you decide to participate in this research study. Your participation is voluntary, and you can decline to participate, or withdraw consent at any time, with no consequences.

Purpose of the Study

This research investigates student interactions with AI chatbots in programming education. The study aims to understand patterns of AI usage and potential implications for learning outcomes.

Study Procedures

Your participation will involve:

  • Completing demographic questions
  • Answering programming-related questions
  • Interacting with an AI chatbot
  • Providing feedback through surveys

The total time commitment is approximately 45 minutes.

Confidentiality

Responses are pseudonymised using a random participant code; they are not anonymous. Your responses will be kept confidential and used only for research purposes.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.

What is recorded and shared

We record your survey answers, selected answers, timing, changes to selections, chat messages and replies, counts of copying and pasting, and how often and how long you leave the study page during a question. UZH does not ask for your name, email, or student ID. Please do not enter personal information in chat.

Your chat messages and the programming problem are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. We do not send your demographic answers or participant code in the model prompt.
