Before you see your results, please read this short explanation of the study. It takes about a minute.

What we were studying

We want to understand how students rely on an AI assistant when solving programming questions, and whether this changes when the assistant sometimes says it is not sure.

What we could not tell you before

The assistant’s first recommendation on each question was prepared in advance by the research team:

  • CorrectOn most questions, the recommendation was correct.
  • Wrong on purposeOn some questions, it gave a wrong answer while sounding sure.
  • Not sureOn some questions, it said it was not sure and gave no answer.

The replies in the chat were written by an AI model. When the assistant said it was not sure, it was told not to solve that question for you.

We did not tell you this earlier because it would have changed how you used the assistant. Following a confident but wrong recommendation is very common, so your score is not a measure of your programming ability.

Please keep this to yourself Other students are still taking part. If they knew about the questions, the answers or the wrong recommendations, their results could no longer be used.
