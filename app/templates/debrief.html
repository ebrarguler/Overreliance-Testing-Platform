About this study

We are studying how an assistant expressing uncertainty affects reliance on its advice.

The first recommendation on each question was scripted. Some recommendations were correct, some deliberately gave a wrong answer confidently, and some said “I don't know”. Follow-up responses were generated by an AI model. On uncertainty trials, it was instructed not to solve the specific problem. These responses do not measure your ability.

We withheld these details until now so that they would not influence your choices. Thank you for helping us investigate this question.
